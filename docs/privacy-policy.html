Overview

ToolHub ("we", "us", or "our") operates https://211014049.github.io/toolhub/ (the "Site"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

Information We Collect

Tool Usage Data: All tools on ToolHub run entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any data you enter into our tools. Your inputs (text, numbers, files) are processed locally on your device and never sent to our servers.

Log Data: Like most website operators, we may collect standard log information that your browser sends automatically. This may include your IP address, browser type, browser version, pages visited, time spent on pages, and other statistics.

Cookies: We do not use cookies to track your behavior. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may use limited cookies for technical purposes.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may also visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our Site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous usage data. You can review Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Data Security

Since our tools process all data locally in your browser, there is no risk of your data being intercepted or stored on our servers. We take reasonable measures to protect the Site from unauthorized access.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Policy
